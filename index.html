How It Works — Math, Not Magic

Syscall sequences are modeled as 32-dimensional normalized frequency fingerprints. Training computes a closed-form decision surface. Inference is a deterministic int8 dot product — identical input, identical output, every time. The model generalizes 51× beyond its training ceiling because normalized frequency ratios are length-invariant by construction.

Step 1: Kernel hook captures syscall trace into ring buffer → Step 2: Extract 32 normalized frequency ratios (closed-form) → Step 3: Int8 dot product classification (34 ns) → Step 4: JSON proof file for CRA Article 13 audit trail
